Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted for Modern Living

Located in the heart of Gurgaon, Real Wood Furniture began with a simple belief: real homes deserve real wood. We reject cheap particle boards and synthetic veneers.

Every dining table, bed frame, and sofa is meticulously sculpted from 100% kiln-dried, seasoned Sheesham (Indian Rosewood) and Grade-A Teak wood. Our master artisans hand-finish each piece to highlight the natural wood grain, giving your interiors genuine warmth and heirloom durability.
